Delete your Sāḍhu account

Fastest way — in the App

Open the Sāḍhu app → Profile → ⋮ menu → Delete account. This deletes your profile, counts, chats, and memberships immediately. You will be signed out and the app will return to its fresh state.

Without the App — email request

Send us a deletion request from the email address of the account (or including your display ID):

Request deletion by email

We process requests within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

Your profile (name, mother's name, display ID), personal counts and streaks, sankalp challenges, chats, and group memberships. Groups you created are handed over to their earliest remaining member so the group's shared yagya totals are preserved for its members. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
