Sāḍhu — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Sāḍhu is a devotional counting app ("the App") that lets you count your daily jaap (mantra recitations) personally and together with groups (sangha). This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and the control you have over it. The App is published by the developer identified in the contact section below ("we", "us").

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address (used as your login) and a password (stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider — we never see it).
  • Profile: your name, your mother's name, and your chosen relation word. These form your public display ID (for example, "Kaushalya Nandan Ram"), which is how other devotees see you inside groups and chats. Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Your counting: the mantras you count, your daily counts, streaks, and the amounts you choose to contribute to a group's shared total. Your personal counts are private to you; contributions are visible to the members of that group.
  • Messages: the content of direct (one-to-one) chats and of text-mode group chats, visible only to their participants/members. In a "naam mode" group chat, whatever you type is replaced by the group's chosen naam before storage — the typed text is never stored or shown.
  • Safety data: if you block or report someone, we store the block/report with your user identifiers to operate these safety features.
  • Local storage: the App keeps a copy of your data on your device so it works offline; signing out removes it from that device.

2. What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no analytics SDKs, no sale of data, no third-party marketing.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or file access.
  • No passwords in readable form — ever.

3. Why we process data

Only to provide the App: creating your account and display ID, keeping your counts, showing group totals you take part in, delivering your chats, and keeping the community safe (blocking and reporting). The legal basis is the performance of the service you requested, and your consent where required.

4. Where data is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted in the Singapore region, protected by row-level security so each account can access only its own data and the group data it belongs to. Data is encrypted in transit.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account, which you confirm at sign-up.

6. Community safety

Chats are for devotees to connect respectfully. Every chat offers in-app reporting and blocking. Reports are reviewed by the app owner, who may remove content and suspend accounts that harass or abuse others. Naam-mode chats can only ever contain the group's chosen naam.

7. Your rights and deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the App: Profile → ⋮ menu → Delete account. This removes your profile, counts, chats, and membership records (groups you created are handed over to their earliest remaining member so shared totals are not lost). You may also request deletion without the App via our account deletion page. Deletion is permanent.

8. Data retention

We keep your data only while your account exists. When you delete your account, personal data is removed from our database. Safety reports may be retained a short while longer to investigate abuse.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.
